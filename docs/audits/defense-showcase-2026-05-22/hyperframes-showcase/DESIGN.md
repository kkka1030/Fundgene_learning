## Style Prompt

FundGene defense showcase uses a light, premium financial-workbench visual language: calm Apple-like materials, translucent white surfaces, precise blue accents, restrained depth, and quiet motion. The video should feel like a polished product demonstration for a beginner-investor agent command center, not a marketing splash page or generic SaaS ad.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f5f9ff`
- Ink: `#0f172a`
- Muted ink: `#64748b`
- Primary blue: `#0071e3`
- Soft blue: `#dceeff`
- Soft green: `#46d0ac`
- Safety amber: `#ffb020`
- Hairline: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`

## Typography

- Chinese/system UI: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Heiti SC`, `system-ui`, sans-serif
- Numeric/data accent: `SF Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace
- Headlines use large, confident weights with tight but readable spacing. Body text is sparse and secondary.

## Motion

- Calm premium movement: focus-pull fades, short y-offset entrances, subtle card lift, gentle scale on chapter cards.
- Transitions should feel continuous, not flashy: crossfades, translucent washes, and slow brand-panel reveals.
- UI footage remains stable and readable; motion must not imply new app behavior that was not recorded.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not cover important UI with subtitles or large labels.
- Do not add stock footage, fake financial charts, or unrelated decorative scenes.
- Do not use dark neon, cyberpunk, purple gradients, or busy particle backgrounds.
- Do not make chapter pages wordy.
- Do not stretch or distort the recorded UI footage.
